STEP 01 / HOW IT WORKS

Tell us what you need.

Send your floor plan, project location, approximate area, intended use and the product categories involved. We discuss the scope before starting detailed design.

Output: an initial project brief and clear next steps.

Fill in our project enquiry form ↗Send floor plan via WhatsApp ↗Attach your drawing in WhatsApp; files are not uploaded by the website form yet.
STEP 02 / HOW IT WORKS

Refine drawings and technical requirements.

Design work is charged by project area. We work through cabinetry dimensions, materials, hardware, appliance clearances and site interfaces, then document requested revisions.

Output: coordinated drawings and recorded technical decisions.

STEP 03 / HOW IT WORKS

One approved set of documents.

The client signs off on the final master drawings, bill of materials (BOM), specifications, agreed sourcing responsibilities and quotation before production is released.

Output: a documented master plan and specification approval.

STEP 04 / HOW IT WORKS

Confirm the order and payment.

After the design, scope, quotation and schedule are agreed, the order is confirmed with a 50% advance payment. Any agreed design-fee refund is addressed in the commercial terms.

Output: confirmed order scope and manufacturing schedule.

STEP 05 / HOW IT WORKS

Manufacture and check the order.

The stated manufacturing period is 35–40 days. This does not include design revisions, transportation, customs clearance or local installation. Quality and packing checks follow the agreed scope.

Output: factory-completed items with the agreed inspection and packing records.

STEP 06 / HOW IT WORKS

Complete payment and arrange shipment.

The remaining 50% is due upon completion of the ordered items at the factory. Shipping is FOB unless DDP is available for the confirmed destination; documentation and import responsibilities are agreed case by case.

Output: a defined shipping and handover arrangement.

PROJECT ESSENTIALS / PRODUCTION

Manufacturing period: 35–40 days

GNORD's stated manufacturing period is 35–40 days for the agreed factory order.

  • Design development, technical revisions and approval happen before manufacturing.
  • Final timing depends on the approved specifications, quantities and order conditions.
  • Transit, import clearance and installation are separate and are not included in this manufacturing period.

We will confirm the applicable production schedule and handover milestones in your order documents.
PROJECT ESSENTIALS / PAYMENT

50% advance · 50% on factory completion

  • 50% advance: Due when the order is confirmed.
  • Remaining 50%: Due when the ordered items are completed at the factory.
  • Order specification, drawing sign-off and any design-fee credit or refund arrangements are documented in the agreed contract.

These are GNORD's standard payment milestones. Any agreed adjustments should be recorded in the quotation or contract.
PROJECT ESSENTIALS / SHIPPING

FOB and selected DDP destinations

FOB is the standard arrangement for destinations where GNORD does not offer DDP. DDP door-to-door service is available only for selected countries, subject to confirmation for the order.

We are familiar with standards, compliance and import-document requirements across many markets. Requirements depend on the product, destination and importer; applicable certification, logistics scope, local obligations and responsibilities should be checked before order confirmation.

Tell us your destination country and planned product categories. We will advise which delivery scope can be offered for your project.

About Us

Our Vision

Gnord Strular is a leading manufacturer of custom cabinetry and wardrobe solutions based in China. We specialize in creating high-quality, custom-made modular furniture for clients across India, Russia, the Middle East, Europe, Australia, and the United States. Our focus on excellence and attention to detail has made us a trusted partner for interior designers, furniture retailers, and homeowners worldwide. We use advanced manufacturing techniques and state-of-the-art equipment to deliver products that exceed expectations.

We believe that premium interior solutions should not be a luxury of the few, but a standard for the modern home. Specializing in modular kitchens, bespoke wardrobes, and integrated cabinetry, we provide whole-house customization that balances high-end European aesthetics with the unparalleled efficiency of advanced factory-direct manufacturing.

Engineering Excellence

Our products are more than furniture; they are precision-engineered systems. Sourced from our state-of-the-art production facilities in China, every cabinet is a result of CNC-driven accuracy and meticulous material selection.

  • Eco-Conscious Standards: We exclusively utilize E0 and ENF-grade formaldehyde-free boards to ensure a healthy indoor environment.
  • Global Hardware Integration: By partnering with industry leaders like Blum, Hettich, Higold and DTC, we guarantee that every hinge, runner, and lift system performs flawlessly for a lifetime.
  • Surface Innovation: From anti-fingerprint PET films to 7-layer piano lacquer paint finishes, our surfaces are tested for moisture resistance, heat stability, and scratch durability.

The Gnord Strular Difference: Modular Flexibility

Unlike traditional "off-the-shelf" retailers, we offer a fully customizable design ecosystem. Whether you are looking for a minimalist Japandi kitchen, a transitional Shaker-style pantry, or a luxury floor-to-ceiling wine display, our modular components can be adapted to any architectural layout.

We bridge the gap between bespoke carpentry and industrial scale, offering you:

  • Direct-to-Consumer Value: Eliminating middle-man markups through a streamlined supply chain.
  • Design-First Approach: A massive library of 100+ textures, wood grains, and sintered stone finishes.
  • Global Reliability: Expertly packed and shipped systems designed for easy on-site assembly and perfect alignment.

Our Commitment

Based on the principles of Gnord (representing strength and northern minimalist roots) and Strular (structure and modularity), our mission is to provide architects, designers, and homeowners with a seamless interior solution that is as functional as it is beautiful.
