Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This notice explains how GNORD Strular Furniture & Interior Solution handles information submitted through this website and how optional website analytics works.

Information you choose to send

If you contact GNORD through our enquiry form or email, you may provide your name, contact information, location, project requirements, messages and optional project drawings or other attachments. We use this information to review your enquiry, respond to questions, discuss project requirements and prepare quotations or related communications.

Website enquiries are transmitted using our email delivery service, Resend, to contactgnord@gmail.com. If you provide drawings, please avoid including personal information that is not needed for your project.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand website visits and how visitors interact with our pages. The Google Analytics tag is not loaded until you choose Allow analytics. If you decline, you can continue using the website normally.

We store your analytics preference locally in your browser so you do not need to answer on every page. You can change your choice whenever you wish using the Cookie settings control below.

External websites and service providers

Links to WhatsApp, social platforms and related GNORD websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Our website is hosted by Vercel, and the services used to host, deliver and analyze website interactions may process technical or enquiry information in other countries.

Questions and data requests

If you have questions about an enquiry, wish to correct information you sent, or want to request its deletion, email contactgnord@gmail.com. Please identify the relevant enquiry so we can review your request.
